Unsafe Browser: you are NOT anonymous here

This browser goes straight onto the local network, not through Tor or PIA. The network (and the sites you open) see your real connection.

Use it only to sign in to a Wi-Fi network (hotel, café, airport): the sign-in page usually appears when you open any plain http:// address. The second tab does that. Once you're signed in, close this browser: Tor or PIA connects by itself.

Nothing is kept: every start is an empty browser, and it's gone when you close it.
